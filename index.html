Who We Are

We Started Because Kids Needed More

C.H.I.L.D. was started because the kids in Rantoul needed more. More support, more people in their corner, more chances to be seen. So Belinda Moffett got to work. That's still what we do.

  • C Children are at the center of everything we do.
  • H Have the potential to achieve greatness.
  • I Intelligent minds that need nurturing & opportunity.
  • L Life is full of choices, and we help youth make good ones.
  • D Decisions shaped by guidance, community, and love.
What We Do

Real Programs for Real Kids

No fluff. Just things that actually help: supplies, stages, and people who care.

🎒

Back to School Giveaway

Every August we hand out backpacks and school supplies to kids in the Rantoul area. No child should walk into the first day of school without what they need.

Annual · Late Summer
🌟

Talent Show

Kids in Rantoul have real talent. We give them a stage to prove it. Singers, dancers, spoken word. Everyone gets a shot to shine in front of their community.

Seasonal Event
🤝

Mentorship Program

Sometimes a kid just needs an adult who shows up consistently. Our mentors do exactly that: listening, guiding, and being present when it matters most.

Year-Round
Mark Your Calendar

We're Out Here Year-Round

Spring, summer, fall, winter. There's always something happening for Rantoul kids.

Spring

Talent Show & Community Celebration

Young performers take the stage and the whole community comes out to cheer them on. It's one of the best nights of the year.

Summer

Youth Outreach & Engagement

School's out but we don't stop. Mentorship check-ins, community activities, and keeping kids connected all summer long.

August

Back to School Giveaway

Our biggest event of the year. Backpacks, supplies, and smiles, handed out right here in Rantoul before the first bell rings.

Fall / Winter

Holiday Outreach & Mentorship

The holidays can be hard for some families. We make sure kids in our community feel remembered, cared for, and not alone.

Meet the Founder

Belinda Moffett

"I started C.H.I.L.D. because I love this community and I believe in these kids. Somebody had to show up, so I did."

Belinda Moffett is a Rantoul native who saw a need and did something about it. C.H.I.L.D. is personal to her. Built from the ground up with her own hands, her own heart, and the support of people who believe in the same thing she does: that every kid in this city deserves a real shot.

She's not running a program. She's raising a community.
